# Costing

Costing holds the structure every amount is booked and planned against: for a household with a
few houses as much as for a company.

## Four questions for every amount

| Question | Answered by | A house | A company |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whose result is it? | **Profit center** | The house Erlenweg 12 | The division Services |
| Where did the cost arise? | **Cost center** | The ground floor flat, the heating | Assembly, IT |
| What kind of cost is it? | **Cost type** | Energy, insurance, repairs | Personnel, material, rent |
| For which piece of work? | **Order** | Roof renovation 2026 | ERP rollout |

## Centers

Profit and cost centers form one tree. A profit center is at the top or under another profit
center; cost centers sit under profit centers or under other cost centers. Each center shows the
**profit center** its costs count for, worked out from where it stands; moving a center updates
everything under it. Each center has a **responsible** person.

## Cost types

The same list everywhere, so every center can be compared with every other: costs and income,
grouped (running costs, upkeep, financing). **May be charged on** marks what a lease or a
contract lets you pass on (heating, water) or bill to a customer.

## Orders

A piece of work with a start and an end, under a center. An order **adds value** (CAPEX: it
becomes part of an asset and is written off over some years) or is **upkeep or expense** (OPEX:
a cost of the year). Offers, contracts and photos can be attached to it.

An order is **open**, then **done** (Mark done; Reopen brings it back), then **settled**: its
costs are final. Only a Controller settles.

## Spreading keys

A **shared** cost center (the heating, the stairs, IT) is spread over the centers it serves by a
key: its shares per center, in m², units, people or percent. Each share goes to a cost center,
never to the shared one itself.

## Who may do what

- **Controller**: sets up and changes the whole structure.
- **Costing reader**: reads it, to book and plan against it.
