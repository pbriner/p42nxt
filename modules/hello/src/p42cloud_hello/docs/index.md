# Hello

Leave a greeting for everyone to read. You can change or delete your own greetings.

## Greetings

| Field | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Text | What you say, up to 200 characters |
| Language | English, German or French |
| Author | You: set when you leave the greeting |

## Who may do what

The **Greeter** role reads every greeting and leaves new ones. Each greeting can be changed or
deleted only by its author.
