# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- Deleting a partner deletes its contacts with it, after a confirmation that lists them; a
  partner with contracts can't be deleted. Needs the core 0.46.

## 0.1.0

- Partners (companies and people; suppliers, customers, tenants), with address, email, phone,
  website, VAT number and IBAN (its check digits checked), files and comments; their contact
  persons; and contracts (lease, service, supply, insurance) with their period, notice, amount
  and period of payment, the advance for running costs of a lease, files and comments, going
  from draft to in force to ended. Roles Partner manager and Partner reader. Needs the core 0.43.
