# Partners

The companies and people you work with, kept once and used by every module: Finance books with
them, Costing and Purchasing name their suppliers here.

## Partners

A partner is a company or a person, and a **supplier**, a **customer**, a **tenant**, or several
of these. It has an address, email, phone, website, VAT number and IBAN; an IBAN is checked by
its check digits and kept in groups of four. Offers, letters and certificates can be attached;
comments keep the conversation with the partner.

## Contact persons

The people at a partner you deal with, one of them the **main contact**.

## Contracts

A lease, a service or supply contract, an insurance: with its partner, **start** and **end**, the
**notice** in months, the **amount** in its currency and how often it's paid. A lease also has
the **advance for running costs** the tenant pays each period. Attach the signed contract.

A contract is a **draft** until it's **put in force**, and stays on record once it's **ended**.

## Who may do what

- **Partner manager**: keeps partners, contact persons and contracts.
- **Partner reader**: reads them, to book and plan with them.

Addresses, emails, phones and IBANs are personal data.
