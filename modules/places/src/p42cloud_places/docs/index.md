# Places

Where things are, as one tree: **sites** hold **buildings**, buildings **floors** or **rooms**,
floors rooms, rooms **racks**. **Other** fits anywhere, for what doesn't fit these.

Each place shows its whole path ("HQ › Building A › 2nd floor › Server room"), and filtering by
a place includes everything under it. Other modules point to places: where a device stands, a
rented flat, what a task is about.

- A **rack** has a height in units (42 unless you say otherwise), so devices take their
  position in it.
- A place with places under it can't be deleted until they're moved or deleted.
- Places readers see every place; Places managers record them.
